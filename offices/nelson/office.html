Why We Call Maryville Home

A few words from our office about the town we live and work in.

Mountain Views, Every Day

Blount County sits at the foot of the Great Smoky Mountains. Townsend, the Peaceful Side of the Smokies, is a short drive from Maryville, and the Foothills Parkway gives you some of the best views in the South. Hiking, tubing on the Little River and weekend drives through Cades Cove are part of life here.

Hometown Maryville

Maryville and Alcoa feel like real hometowns. Downtown Maryville has local shops and restaurants, Maryville College anchors the community, and the Greenway connects parks and neighborhoods. Strong city and county schools and Friday night football bring people together.

Close To Everything

McGhee Tyson Airport is right next door, downtown Knoxville is about 25 minutes up Alcoa Highway, and Fort Loudoun Lake and the Little Tennessee River are close by. You get small-town living with big-city jobs, healthcare and shopping in easy reach.
