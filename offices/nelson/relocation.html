Moving To Maryville FAQ

Does Tennessee have a state income tax?

No. Tennessee does not tax wages or salaries. Sales tax is higher than in many states, and property taxes in Blount County are modest compared with most metro areas.

How far is Maryville from Knoxville and the airport?

McGhee Tyson Airport is minutes from Maryville, and downtown Knoxville is about 25 minutes up Alcoa Highway.

What is the weather like?

Expect four seasons: warm summers, colorful mountain falls, short winters with occasional snow and early springs.

Is it close to the Great Smoky Mountains?

Yes. Townsend and the park's Cades Cove entrance are about 30 minutes from Maryville, and the Foothills Parkway runs through Walland.

What about healthcare?

Blount Memorial Hospital is in Maryville, and Knoxville's major hospital systems are about 25 minutes away.

Can I buy a home before I move?

Yes. Many of our relocation clients buy after one or two visits, using video tours, remote inspections and e-signatures, and close with a local title company.

How are the schools?

Blount County has Maryville City, Alcoa City and Blount County Schools, plus private options. Zoning follows the property, so we verify assigned schools for each home.

Call us or send a message. We will set up a discovery call, build your area shortlist and start a custom home search the same week.
