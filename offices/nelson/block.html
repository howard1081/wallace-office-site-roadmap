Maryville & Blount County Real Estate FAQs

Quick answers to the questions we hear most about buying, selling and relocating to Maryville and the rest of Blount County.

How far is Maryville from Knoxville and the airport?

Most clients plan on about 25 minutes to downtown Knoxville by Alcoa Highway or Pellissippi Parkway. McGhee Tyson Airport is in Alcoa, about 10 minutes away.

How close is Maryville to the Smoky Mountains?

Townsend and the Great Smoky Mountains National Park entrance are about 30 minutes from downtown Maryville by Highway 321.

How do schools work in Blount County?

There are three public systems: Maryville City Schools, Alcoa City Schools and Blount County Schools. Zoning follows the property, so we verify the exact school for any home.

What should I know about buying a mountain home or cabin?

Check road access and steepness, septic and well, internet, and any short-term rental rules. We help you confirm these before you write an offer.

What should I know about buying on Fort Loudoun Lake?

Shoreline is managed by TVA, so check the dock permit, water depth and flood zone. We'll also confirm sewer or septic and any HOA rules.

How competitive is the Blount County market?

It depends on price range and area. Well-priced homes in popular school zones can move quickly. We use recent Blount County sales to set your strategy.

What do I need to do before selling my Maryville home?

Start with pricing, condition and presentation. We'll do a walk-through, pull current Blount County sales and plan photos and launch timing.

I'm relocating to Maryville from out of state. Where do I start?

Start with your move date, budget and the lifestyle you want. We'll set up a search, send video tours, line up a local lender and plan a tour of Maryville, Alcoa, Louisville and Townsend.
