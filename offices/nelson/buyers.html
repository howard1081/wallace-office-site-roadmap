Maryville Home Buyer FAQ

How much are property taxes in Blount County?

Tennessee taxes homes on 25% of appraised value, and homes inside Maryville or Alcoa city limits pay a city tax on top of the county rate. Rates here are modest compared with most metro areas. We pull the exact tax bill for any home you are considering.

Which school system will my home be in?

Blount County has three systems: Maryville City, Alcoa City and Blount County Schools. Zoning follows the property, not the mailing address, so we verify the assigned schools for each home.

Do many homes here have wells or septic?

Inside Maryville and Alcoa most homes have city water and sewer. Many homes in the county use a private well or septic system, and we help you schedule inspections so you know what you are buying.

How far is Maryville from Knoxville and the Smokies?

Plan on about 25 minutes to downtown Knoxville, about 30 minutes to Townsend and the park entrance, and only minutes to McGhee Tyson Airport.

Can I buy a cabin or short-term rental in Blount County?

Yes, especially around Townsend and Walland. Rules for short-term rentals vary by location and HOA, so we check zoning and restrictions before you make an offer.

How much do I need for a down payment?

Conventional loans start around 3% to 5% down, FHA at 3.5%, and VA and USDA can be zero down in eligible areas. Our local lender partners will tell you exactly what you qualify for.

Is now a good time to buy in Maryville?

It depends on your price range and property type. Well-priced homes in Maryville and Alcoa move quickly; acreage and higher-end homes often give you more time to negotiate. We show you current Blount County numbers so you decide with facts.

I'm moving from out of state. How do I buy without being here?

We set up your search, send live video tours, line up a local lender and inspectors, and plan a tour weekend around Maryville, Alcoa and Townsend so you can compare them in person.
