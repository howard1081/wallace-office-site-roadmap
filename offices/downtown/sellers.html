Downtown Knoxville Home Seller FAQ

How do you price a downtown condo?

We compare recent sales in your building and nearby buildings, then adjust for floor, view, parking, size and HOA fee. We do not rely on online estimates.

Should I fix things before I list?

Some small fixes pay off, like paint, lighting and cleaning. We walk through your home and tell you which projects are worth it and which are not.

How long will it take to sell?

It depends on price, location and condition. We share the latest days-on-market numbers for your area when we meet.

What do I need from my HOA?

Buyers will ask for the HOA rules, budget, recent meeting minutes and any planned assessments. We help you gather them early so the sale does not stall.

Do you use professional photos?

Yes. Every listing gets professional photography, and many get video, drone and 3D tours.

Where will my home be advertised?

On the MLS, Zillow, Realtor.com, wallacetn.com, social media and email to our buyer list, plus the Wallace network of agents across East Tennessee.

What does it cost to sell?

Common costs include the commission, title fees, and any repairs or credits you agree to. We give you a net sheet so you know what you will walk away with.

Can you help me buy my next home too?

Yes. We can help you time your sale and purchase, whether you are moving across Knox County or out of state.
