Downtown Knoxville & Knox County Real Estate FAQs

Quick answers to the questions we hear most about buying, selling and relocating to Downtown Knoxville and the rest of Knox County.

Is it a good idea to buy a condo in downtown Knoxville?

For many buyers, yes. Downtown condos and lofts let you walk to Market Square, the Old City, restaurants and events. Before you buy, compare the HOA fee, what it covers, parking, rental rules and the building's reserve fund. We help you review all of it.

Do I pay both city and county property taxes?

If the home is inside Knoxville city limits, you pay both Knoxville city and Knox County property taxes. Homes outside the city pay only county taxes. Tennessee also has no state income tax on wages.

Which neighborhoods are close to downtown?

The Old City, Fourth & Gill, Old North Knoxville, Happy Holler, Fort Sanders, South Knoxville, Island Home and Sequoyah Hills are all a short drive, bike ride or walk from Gay Street.

What about parking downtown?

Some condos come with a deeded or assigned space, and some do not. Others use nearby city garages with monthly passes. We check the parking for every downtown home so there are no surprises.

How are the schools?

Public schools in the area are run by Knox County Schools, and zoning follows the address. We confirm the assigned schools for every home you look at.

Do you only help buyers and sellers downtown?

No. Downtown is our home base, but we help clients across Knox County and the counties next to it, including Anderson, Blount, Loudon, Roane, Sevier, Jefferson, Grainger and Union.

How far is the airport?

McGhee Tyson Airport (TYS) is in Alcoa, about 20 to 25 minutes south of downtown Knoxville in normal traffic.

How do I find out what my home is worth?

Call our office or send us a message. A Downtown agent will look at recent sales near you and give you a free, no-pressure estimate.
