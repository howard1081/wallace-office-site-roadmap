Downtown Knoxville Home Buyer FAQ

What should I check before buying a downtown condo?

Look at the HOA fee and what it covers, the reserve fund, any special assessments, parking, pet and rental rules, and recent sales in the building. We gather all of it for you.

Are there historic district rules?

Some Knoxville neighborhoods, like Fourth & Gill and Old North Knoxville, are historic overlay districts. Exterior changes may need approval. We tell you if a home is in one before you make an offer.

Do I pay city and county taxes?

Homes inside Knoxville city limits pay both city and Knox County property taxes. Homes outside the city pay county taxes only.

How much do I need for a down payment?

It depends on the loan. Some loans allow as little as 3 to 3.5 percent down, and some condo buildings have lender rules. We connect you with local lenders who know downtown buildings.

How are schools assigned?

Knox County Schools assigns schools by address. We confirm the zoned schools for every home.

Can I buy from out of town?

Yes. We do live video tours, send local notes on each home and handle inspections and closing with e-signatures.

How fast do homes sell?

It changes by area and price. Well-priced homes near downtown can sell quickly, so we set up instant alerts and help you be ready to write an offer.

Do you work outside downtown?

Yes. We help buyers across Knox County and the counties next to it, including Anderson, Blount, Loudon, Roane, Sevier, Jefferson, Grainger and Union.
