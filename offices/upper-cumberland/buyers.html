Why Buyers Choose Cumberland County

Here is what our clients tell us drew them to Crossville, Fairfield Glade, Lake Tansi and the surrounding plateau towns.

Plateau Climate

At nearly 2,000 feet, summers are milder than the valleys below, with four real seasons and clean mountain air.

I-40 Access

Crossville sits right on I-40. Cookeville is about 35 minutes, Knoxville about an hour and 15, and Nashville about two hours.

Lower Cost Of Living

Home prices and property taxes run below most metro areas, and Tennessee has no state income tax on wages.

Golf & Lake Communities

Fairfield Glade and Lake Tansi offer golf courses, lakes, pools and clubs, popular with retirees and second-home buyers.

State Parks Nearby

Cumberland Mountain State Park, Ozone Falls and the Obed Wild and Scenic River are all close to home.

Room To Spread Out

From in-town homes to mountain cabins and acreage, the plateau has space for gardens, horses and workshops.

Crossville Home Buyer FAQ

How much are property taxes in Cumberland County?

Tennessee taxes homes on 25% of appraised value, and homes inside Crossville city limits pay a city tax on top of the county rate. Rates here are modest compared with most metro areas. We pull the exact tax bill for any home you are considering.

What does it cost to live in Fairfield Glade or Lake Tansi?

Both communities charge a monthly or yearly POA fee that funds roads, amenities and common areas. Golf and some extras can cost more. We walk you through the current fees and rules for each community.

Do many homes here have wells or septic?

Yes. Many homes outside town use a private well and septic system. We help you schedule a septic inspection and water test so you know what you are buying.

Which school system will my home be in?

Public schools across the county are run by Cumberland County Schools. Zoning follows the property, not the mailing address, so we verify the assigned schools for each home.

How far is Crossville from Knoxville and Nashville?

Plan on about an hour and 15 minutes to Knoxville, about two hours to Nashville and about 35 minutes to Cookeville, all on I-40. Chattanooga is about an hour and a half.

How much do I need for a down payment?

Conventional loans start around 3% to 5% down, FHA at 3.5%, and VA and USDA can be zero down; much of rural Cumberland County is USDA-eligible. Our local lender partners will tell you exactly what you qualify for.

Is now a good time to buy in Crossville?

It depends on your price range and property type. Well-priced homes in town and in the planned communities move quickly; acreage and higher-end homes often give you more time to negotiate. We show you current Cumberland County numbers so you decide with facts.

I'm moving from out of state. How do I buy without being here?

Many of our clients relocate from other states. We set up your search, send live video tours, line up a local lender and inspectors, and plan a tour weekend around Crossville, Fairfield Glade and Lake Tansi so you can compare them in person.
