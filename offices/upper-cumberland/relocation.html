Moving To Crossville FAQ

Does Tennessee have a state income tax?

No. Tennessee does not tax wages or salaries. Sales tax is higher than in many states, and property taxes in Cumberland County are modest compared with most metro areas.

How far is Crossville from Knoxville, Nashville and the airports?

Knoxville is about an hour and 15 minutes east on I-40 and Nashville about two hours west. Most people fly in and out of Knoxville's McGhee Tyson or Nashville International.

What is the weather like?

Crossville sits at nearly 2,000 feet, so summers are milder than in Knoxville or Nashville. Winters are short, with occasional snow on the plateau.

Are Fairfield Glade and Lake Tansi age-restricted?

No. Both are popular with retirees but are not age-restricted, and both have property owners associations with monthly or yearly fees.

What about healthcare?

Cumberland Medical Center is in Crossville, and larger regional hospitals are in Cookeville and Knoxville.

Can I buy a home before I move?

Yes. Many of our relocation clients buy after one or two visits, using video tours, remote inspections and e-signatures, and close with a local title company.

How are the schools?

Public schools are run by Cumberland County Schools, with private and faith-based options in the area. Zoning follows the property, so we verify assigned schools for each home.
