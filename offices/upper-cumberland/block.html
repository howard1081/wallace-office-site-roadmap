Crossville & Cumberland County Real Estate FAQs

Quick answers to the questions we hear most about buying, selling and relocating to Crossville and the rest of Cumberland County.

Why do so many people move to Crossville?

Buyers come for the cooler plateau climate, lower costs, golf and lake communities, and the location on I-40 about halfway between Knoxville and Nashville. Many are retirees, but we also work with families and remote workers.

How far is Crossville from Knoxville, Cookeville and Nashville?

Most clients plan on about an hour to Knoxville, around 40 minutes to Cookeville and roughly two hours to Nashville, all by I-40. Times vary by which side of the county you live on.

Is Fairfield Glade right for me?

Fairfield Glade is a large planned community with several golf courses, lakes, trails and clubs. Expect property owners' association dues on top of your home cost. We'll walk you through the fees, the neighborhoods and resale trends.

What should I know about buying land on the plateau?

Check septic soil tests, well or water service, road frontage, deed restrictions and internet options. We help you confirm these before you write an offer.

How do schools work in Cumberland County?

Cumberland County Schools serves the county, with high schools including Cumberland County High and Stone Memorial. Zoning follows the property, so we verify the exact school for any home you're considering.

How competitive is the Cumberland County market?

It depends on price range and area. Well-priced homes in popular communities can move quickly, while larger acreage and higher-end homes usually allow more time. We use recent local sales to set your strategy.

What do I need to do before selling my Crossville home?

Start with pricing, condition and presentation. We'll do a walk-through, pull current Cumberland County sales and plan photos and launch timing.

I'm relocating to Crossville from out of state. Where do I start?

Start with your move date, budget and the lifestyle you want. We'll set up a search, send video tours, line up a local lender and plan a tour of Crossville, Fairfield Glade and Lake Tansi.
