Moving To Lenoir City FAQ

Does Tennessee have a state income tax?

No. Tennessee has no state income tax on wages or retirement income. The state sales tax is higher than average, and Loudon County property taxes are low compared with most of the places our clients move from.

How far is Lenoir City from Knoxville and the airport?

About 25 to 35 minutes to downtown Knoxville on I-40/I-75, 15 minutes to Farragut and Turkey Creek shopping, and about 20 minutes to McGhee Tyson Airport (TYS) via Highway 321 and Pellissippi Parkway.

What is the weather like?

Four seasons without the extremes: summer highs in the upper 80s, mild falls and springs, and winters with occasional light snow that rarely sticks for long. Lake season runs roughly April through October.

Is Tellico Village age-restricted?

No. It is popular with retirees and active adults, but families live there too. Every property pays a monthly POA assessment for roads, recreation centers, pools and marinas; golf and yacht club memberships are optional.

What about healthcare?

Fort Loudoun Medical Center is in Lenoir City, and Knoxville's major hospital systems (Covenant, UT Medical Center, Tennova) are within 30 minutes. Tellico Village and Lenoir City also have local clinics and specialists.

Can I buy a home before I move?

Yes, and most of our relocation clients do. We handle video tours, remote inspections and e-signature closings, and can coordinate a leaseback or later possession date if you need time to sell your current home.

How are the schools?

Lenoir City Schools serves homes inside the city limits; Loudon County Schools serves the rest of the county. Farragut's highly rated Knox County schools are 15 minutes east. We confirm the zoned school for every home you consider.
