Lenoir City Home Buyer FAQ

How much are property taxes in Loudon County?

Loudon County's rate is among the lowest in the Knoxville area, and Tennessee taxes homes on 25% of appraised value. Homes inside Lenoir City or Loudon city limits pay a city tax on top of the county rate. We pull the exact tax bill for any home you are considering.

What does it cost to live in Tellico Village?

Every Tellico Village property pays a monthly POA assessment that funds the roads, recreation centers, pools and marinas. Golf, yacht club and some amenities are optional add-ons. We walk you through the current fees and the differences between the Village's neighborhoods.

Can I build a dock on a lakefront lot?

Shoreline on Fort Loudoun and Tellico Lake is managed by TVA. Some lots have dock permits already, some can get one and some cannot. We confirm the permit status, water depth at winter pool and any shoreline restrictions before you write an offer.

Which school system will my home be in?

Homes inside the city limits are served by Lenoir City Schools; the rest of the county, including Loudon, Greenback and Philadelphia, is Loudon County Schools. Zoning follows the property, not the mailing address, so we verify the assigned school for each home.

How long is the commute to Knoxville?

Plan on about 15 minutes to Farragut and Turkey Creek, 25 to 35 minutes to downtown Knoxville on I-40/I-75, and about 30 minutes to Oak Ridge on Highway 95. Tellico Village and Loudon add roughly 10 to 15 minutes.

How much do I need for a down payment?

Conventional loans start around 3% to 5% down, FHA at 3.5%, and VA and USDA can be zero down; much of rural Loudon County is USDA-eligible. Our local lender partners will tell you exactly what you qualify for before you start touring.

Is now a good time to buy in Lenoir City?

It depends on your price range and property type. Well-priced in-town homes and lakefront under the local median move quickly; higher-end lake and golf homes usually give you more time to negotiate. We will show you current Loudon County days-on-market and sale-to-list numbers so you decide with facts.

I'm moving from out of state. How do I buy without being here?

Most of our relocation clients buy after one or two visits. We set up your search, send live video tours, line up a local lender and inspectors, and plan a tour weekend around Lenoir City, Tellico Village, Loudon and Greenback so you can compare them in person.
